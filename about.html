About LUNMONT / 关于我们

Yiwu, Zhejiang, China · Hair Accessories Manufacturing & Export

LUNMONT is the export brand of Yiwu Cenova Import and Export Co., Ltd. (义乌市玥岑进出口有限公司), based in Yiwu, Zhejiang, China. We specialize in hair accessories — acetate hair clips, claw clips and fashion hair accessories — supplying wholesalers, retailers and distributors worldwide.
