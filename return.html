本政策适用于通过 lunmont.asia 询盘、洽谈并达成的商品交易，由义乌市玥岑进出口有限公司（统一社会信用代码 91330782MAK7FM3R1M）运营。下单即视为同意本政策。

1. Scope

This policy applies to transactions initiated through lunmont.asia and concluded with Yiwu Cenova Import and Export Co., Ltd. (Unified Social Credit Code 91330782MAK7FM3R1M). Placing an order constitutes acceptance of this policy.

2. 商品与贸易方式

本网站主营发饰等饰品的批发与出口，面向企业采购方。多数商品按订单生产或配货，设有最小起订量（MOQ）；网站所示价格均为出厂批发参考价（人民币 CNY），最终成交价以书面报价单 / 合同为准。

2. Products & trade model

We primarily wholesale and export hair accessories and other fashion accessories to business buyers. Most items are produced or allocated to order and subject to a minimum order quantity (MOQ). Prices shown are ex-factory wholesale reference prices in CNY; the final price is confirmed by a written quotation or contract.

3. 退换货条件

我们重视品质，仅在以下情形支持退换：

  • 收货后 7 个工作日内提出，且商品存在出厂质量缺陷（如断裂、脱胶、明显色差）；
  • 提供清晰的问题照片或视频作为凭证；
  • 商品保持未使用、原包装与吊牌完整。

非质量问题的退换（如选错款式、改变主意）需双方另行协商，通常不设无理由退换。

3. Return & exchange conditions

We stand behind our quality and accept returns/exchanges only in these cases:

  • Claim raised within 7 business days of receipt, for factory defects (e.g. breakage, de-bonding, obvious color deviation);
  • Clear photos or video of the defect are provided as evidence;
  • Items are unused, with original packaging and tags intact.

Non-quality returns (e.g. wrong style chosen, change of mind) are by mutual agreement only; unconditional returns are generally not offered.

4. 退款流程

经确认属质量问题的订单，我们提供以下处理：补发、换货或退款。若退款，将在确认后 5–10 个工作日内按原支付渠道退回（或抵扣后续订单）。退款金额以双方确认的瑕疵商品数量与金额为限。

4. Refund process

For confirmed quality issues we offer replacement, exchange or refund. Where a refund applies, it is returned via the original payment channel (or credited to a future order) within 5–10 business days after confirmation. The refund covers only the confirmed defective quantity and amount agreed by both parties.

5. 不支持退换的情形

  • 按客户要求定制 / 个性化的商品；
  • 已拆封使用、人为损坏或影响二次销售的；
  • 因买家保管、运输（非我方承运）导致的损耗；
  • 超过 7 个工作日未提出的质量异议。

5. Exclusions

  • Items customized or personalized to the buyer's specification;
  • Goods that have been opened, used, or damaged by the buyer, or are not in re-sellable condition;
  • Loss or damage arising from the buyer's own storage or shipping (not carried by us);
  • Quality claims raised after the 7-business-day window.

6. 运费、税费与关税

退换货运费由责任方承担：质量问题由我方承担，非质量问题由买家承担。跨境订单的进口关税、增值税及清关费用通常由买家承担，请以目的国规定为准。

6. Shipping, tax & customs

Return shipping is borne by the responsible party: by us for quality issues, by the buyer otherwise. For cross-border orders, import duties, VAT and customs clearance costs are normally borne by the buyer, subject to the destination country's rules.

7. 争议解决与联系方式

如对本政策或订单有异议，请先通过下方联系方式沟通，我们将在 15 个工作日内响应并协商解决方案。协商不成，提交公司所在地（浙江省义乌市）有管辖权的人民法院处理。

7. Disputes & contact

For any dispute about this policy or an order, please contact us below first; we will respond and seek a resolution within 15 business days. If no settlement is reached, the matter shall be submitted to the people's court with jurisdiction at our location (Yiwu, Zhejiang, China).
