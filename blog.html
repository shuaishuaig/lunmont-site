Sourcing Guide for Hair Accessories 采购指南

Practical notes for wholesale buyers · 面向批发买家的实用说明

1. Choose the right material

Acetate is durable and holds color well; it is a common choice for claw clips and everyday hair accessories. Metal clips suit minimal designs; pearl and rhinestone pieces are typically selected for gift and occasion lines. Ask for material and finish details before placing a bulk order.

醋酸材质耐用、着色稳定，常用于抓夹与日常发饰；金属夹适合简约款式；珍珠/镶钻款多用于礼品与场合配饰。批量下单前请先确认材质与工艺细节。

2. Plan a low-risk first order

Start with a small mixed order across a few SKUs to check quality, packaging and sell-through. Confirm item number, quantity and destination port in your inquiry so we can quote accurately. Mixed designs and low minimum order quantities are available for trial orders.

首次合作建议小批量、多款混搭，先验证品质、包装与动销。询盘时请注明款号、数量与目的港，便于准确报价。试单可混款、起订量灵活。

3. Custom designs and branding

Custom colors, finishes and packaging can be discussed for established buyers. Share reference images or a specification with your inquiry. We will confirm feasibility and any additional cost before production.

长期客户可讨论定制颜色、工艺与包装。请随询盘提供参考图或规格说明；我们会在生产前确认可行性与附加费用。

4. Export and shipment

We export from Yiwu, China and can quote based on your destination port. Shipping method, lead time and documentation are confirmed per order. Contact us for a quotation based on your actual requirements.

货物自中国义乌出口，可按您的目的港报价。运输方式、交期与单证按订单确认。欢迎按实际需求索取报价。
