Production & Quality / 生产与品质

Yiwu, Zhejiang, China · Acetate Hair Clips & Hair Accessories

Our hair accessories are sourced and finished through Yiwu's established accessories supply chain. We work with manufacturing partners for acetate and metal components and handle selection, quality inspection, packing and export from Yiwu. For buyers, this means market-tested designs, flexible quantities and one contact point for a mixed catalog.

How an order moves / 订单流程

  1. Design selection / 选款Choose from our catalog by style, size and color, or send a sample / drawing for custom development.
  2. Sample confirmation / 打样确认We confirm color, material and finish before bulk production.
  3. Quality inspection / 品质检验Each batch is checked for finish, color consistency and clip strength before packing.
  4. Packing & export / 包装出口Export-grade packing and documentation for shipment worldwide from Yiwu.

Quality control / 品质把控

We focus on the details buyers care about: smooth acetate edges, reliable clip mechanisms, consistent colour between batches and secure packing. If a design does not meet the agreed sample, we do not ship it as finished stock.
